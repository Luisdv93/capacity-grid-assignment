# Team capacity

Terms for comparing assigned work with a person's available weekly hours.

## Language

**Week**:
A period from Monday through Sunday. Working days are Monday through Friday; public holidays are not excluded.

**Weekly capacity**:
A person's current available hours per week. This value applies to every displayed week, including past weeks; it is not historical capacity.
_Avoid_: Historical availability

**Effective range**:
The full weeks that contain the selected start and end dates, from the first Monday through the last Sunday.
_Avoid_: Selected range when referring to the expanded dates

**Assigned hours**:
The sum of assigned work on working days within a week. Overlapping assignments all contribute to this total.

**Over-allocation**:
Assigned hours that exceed weekly capacity. Positive assigned hours with zero capacity also constitute over-allocation.
_Avoid_: Percentage when capacity is zero
