# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Managers who compare assigned work with each person's weekly capacity and identify over-commitment before a week starts.

## Product Purpose

A capacity grid, intended to sit beside a separate time-logging timeline. Only capacity is in scope for this assignment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed in the design interview: Monday-based weeks, weekday-only allocation, full-week range expansion, and current weekly capacity applied to every week. Managers can edit capacity and see confirmed figures without a page reload. Start with three weeks; support selected ranges of up to two years and virtualized rows. Loading, errors, late responses, keyboard access, and failed saves are part of the feature.

The Go, PostgreSQL, React, TypeScript, and Compose stack is fixed. Do not change the schema, seed, run environment, or human-owned DECISIONS.md. No authentication is included; the API is a local demonstration.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md defines the assignment. db/schema.sql and db/seed.sql are fixed inputs. GLOSSARY.md records confirmed domain terms. Performance and behavior require running verification; no performance claims are established.
